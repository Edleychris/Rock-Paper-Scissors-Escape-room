Escape the Evil AI!

Mwahahaha! You are trapped inside my digital fortress, human. Branko cannot rescue you now. Only your logic can help you escape!

How to play

  • The game uses pop-up boxes. Type a number (or a keyword) and press OK.
  • Capital letters and extra spaces don't matter. Invalid answers never cost you a move.
  • You have a limited number of moves before the AI's security sweep finds you.
  • Pressing Cancel on any question ends the game safely.
  • Your choices matter: items you find and things you change are remembered.

Please open the console first (recommended)

The game also writes a Fortress Log (map, events and the AI's taunts) to the browser console. It helps you keep track of what happened.

  • Windows / Linux: press F12 or Ctrl+Shift+J, then click the Console tab.
  • Mac: press Cmd+Option+J (Chrome) or Cmd+Option+C (Safari, after enabling the Develop menu).

The game can still be completed without the console.
